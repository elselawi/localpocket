# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in LocalPocket to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about the code and respectful toward the person.
- Assume competence and good faith; ask before lecturing.
- Give and accept technical criticism gracefully. Critique the change, not the
  contributor.
- Respect that maintainers review in their own time, and that "no" is a
  complete answer to a feature request.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Dismissing a report with "just a joke" or "just a question".

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages, and the review comments on them — and whenever an
individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately
to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting channel](https://github.com/elselawi/localpocket/security/advisories/new).
All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially; reporters' privacy and
safety are prioritized.

Maintainers will respond with whatever action they judge proportionate —
a private warning, a request to edit or delete a comment, a temporary or
permanent ban from project spaces, or removal of a contribution. Maintainers
who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face the same
consequences as other participants.
